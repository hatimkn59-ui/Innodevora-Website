01

Who is responsible

The data controller is Innodevora, the publisher of this website. For any question or request about your data, contact contact@innodevora.com.

Postal and legal-entity details are available in the Legal Notice. This notice applies only to the AI assistant on this website; the rest of the site can be used without chatting.

02

What is collected

Always, when you chat

  • The messages you send and the assistant's replies.
  • Anonymous project context: interests, project type, budget range and timeline when you mention them.
  • A random visitor identifier and a technical session cookie.
  • Salted, non-reversible IP hashes for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Only after you consent

  • Your name, email address, company, role and country.
  • The project description you choose to share.
  • A lead score and the reasons for it, so the team can prioritise relevant enquiries.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment card details, bank details, government identifiers, health data or other sensitive information. The assistant is designed not to ask for them.

Before consent, direct identifiers such as email addresses and phone numbers are redacted from stored messages. They are not added to a contact profile until you use the consent button. Innodevora never infers your name, company or location from your IP address or device.

03

Consent comes first

You can discuss a project anonymously. When saving contact details would be useful, the assistant offers a clear consent card. Nothing identifiable is saved to your lead profile until you actively choose Allow & continue.

Saying “yes” in a chat message is not treated as consent — the consent button is the only way the server records it. You can withdraw consent at any time from the options menu. Withdrawing removes your name, email, company, role, country, free-form project description and identifiable content from stored messages.

04

Why it is used

  • To answer you and keep the context of the conversation.
  • To understand a project enquiry and prepare a useful human follow-up.
  • To prioritise leads based on stated intent, not personal characteristics.
  • To protect the service against spam, abuse and security incidents.
  • To meet legal obligations and handle privacy requests.

Innodevora does not sell your data, use it for unrelated advertising, or run cross-site advertising trackers on this site. The assistant is not used for automated decisions that produce legal effects.

Where GDPR applies, identifiable lead data is processed on the basis of your consent. Anonymous conversation context and security records are processed on the basis of Innodevora's legitimate interest in answering enquiries and protecting the service.

05

AI processing

Your messages may be sent to an AI provider configured by Innodevora so that a reply can be generated. The provider processes the text to produce the response; it does not receive provider keys from your browser because all AI calls happen on Innodevora's server.

The exact provider and its data-processing terms depend on the deployment. You can ask Innodevora for the current provider details at contact@innodevora.com. The assistant is instructed not to request sensitive information and never to invent company facts, prices, guarantees or availability.

The assistant can make mistakes. Important commercial terms, quotes, availability and contractual commitments must always be confirmed by a human.

06

Cookies and security

The assistant uses strictly necessary cookies to keep your chat session secure and to remember your consent choice. They are HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict and, where the site is served over HTTPS, Secure. They are not advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, so no consent banner is needed for them.

Security measures include server-side sessions, CSRF protection, same-origin request checks, request-size limits, rate limiting, prepared database queries, strict security headers, role-based admin access and audit logs. Audit records do not contain your chat messages.

07

How long it is kept

By default, visitor profiles are kept for up to 180 days and conversations for up to 365 days. Innodevora can shorten these periods in the admin settings. Expired records are purged automatically, and an administrator can trigger a purge manually.

You can delete everything immediately using Delete my data in the chat options menu. This removes your profile, conversations, messages and consent history. If you also want any email correspondence deleted, contact contact@innodevora.com.

08

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before the withdrawal.

Use the options menu in the chat for immediate deletion or consent withdrawal, or email contact@innodevora.com. Innodevora may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. If you are in the EU/EEA and are not satisfied with the response, you can complain to your local supervisory authority.

The assistant is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact Innodevora so it can be deleted.
